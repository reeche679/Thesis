Help & Documentation

Learn the current workflow of the Program of Study Generation System, from setup to generation, preview, and history management.

Getting Started

Follow these steps before generating your POS:

  1. Log in using your SR-CODE and password.
  2. On Home, upload your profile photo and select your Academic Year.
  3. Click Generate POS, then set your Last Semester Enrolled.
  4. Enter grades, generate, then review and download from Preview POS.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I generate my Program of Study?

Open Generate POS, choose your Last Semester Enrolled, click OK, then fill in grades for all visible courses. Click Generate POS to produce your schedule.

Can I edit courses after generating?

Yes. On Preview POS, use Edit to drag ungraded courses between future semesters, then click Apply to save changes.

How do I download my Program of Study?

After generating, go to Preview POS, select the required signatories, then click Download PDF. Downloading also stores a record in History.

Why does generation fail sometimes?

Common reasons are: missing grades in visible tables, missing semester selection, or failed-unit policy checks. The 50%/75% failed-unit block applies to semesters with at least 10 total units. For unresolved issues, contact support.

How does History loading work?

Loading a History row restores that POS back into Generate POS so you can continue from the loaded setup. The semester dropdown can extend beyond 5th year only when the loaded POS includes those semesters.

System Features

  • Home: Manage student profile photo, view account info, and set Academic Year.
  • Generate POS: Configure regular/irregular flow, add courses for irregular semesters, validate, clear grades, delete current POS data, and generate schedule output.
  • Preview POS: Review generated tables, switch solver output mode (No Petition / With Petition), edit movable courses, manage signatories, and download PDF.
  • History: View saved POS records, load a previous record back to Generate, delete individual rows, or delete all history.
