About the Program of Study Generation System

A thesis project developed for Batangas State University – The National Engineering University.

Thesis Overview

The Program of Study Generation System is designed to assist students and academic advisers of Batangas State University in visualizing and organizing the sequence of courses to be taken throughout the degree program. The system aims to provide a clear and structured representation of the student's academic pathway while considering curriculum rules and university policies.

This interface presents the user-facing components of the system, focusing on clarity, usability, and adherence to the formal and academic branding of the university.

The system interface follows the institutional colors, tone, and formal style of the university, highlighting its identity as the National Engineering University and supporting its mission to provide quality engineering and technology education.

System Notes

  • This version of the interface focuses on layout and user experience only.
  • No actual student records, grades, or curriculum rules are processed in this UI mock-up.
  • Backend processing, algorithms, and database integration are handled separately in the Django application.
