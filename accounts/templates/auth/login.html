The Program of Study Generation System helps you create and manage your Program of Study (POS) for your degree. It automatically organizes subjects based on your curriculum, year level, and academic rules. This system reduces manual errors, saves time, and ensures that your study plan follows university policies.

  • Use Create Account to register with your SR-Code and BSU email.
  • Use Login to sign in with your SR-Code and password.
