Help & Documentation

Find answers to common questions and learn how to use the Program of Study Generation System.

Getting Started

Welcome to the Program of Study Generation System. This system helps you visualize and organize your academic course sequence throughout your degree program.

  1. Log in using your SR-Code and password
  2. View your current academic information on the Home page
  3. Generate your Program of Study for the current term
  4. Preview and download your Program of Study

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I generate my Program of Study?

Navigate to the "Generate POS" page, review your student profile, and select grades for your enrolled courses. Click "Generate POS" to create your Program of Study.

Can I edit my Program of Study after generating it?

You can generate a new Program of Study at any time. Previous versions are saved in your History for reference.

How do I download my Program of Study?

After generating your Program of Study, go to the "Preview POS" page and click the "Download PDF" button to save a copy.

What should I do if I encounter an error?

If you experience any technical issues, please contact system support at pos-support@g.batstate-u.edu.ph or visit the Contact Us page.

System Features

  • Home: View your student information and current academic term
  • Generate POS: Create your Program of Study by selecting course grades
  • Preview POS: Review and download your generated Program of Study
  • History: Access previously generated Programs of Study

Need More Help?

If you need additional assistance, please visit the Contact Us page or reach out to your academic adviser.
