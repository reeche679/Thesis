Privacy Policy

Batangas State University – The National Engineering University · Program of Study Generation System

Introduction

Batangas State University (BSU) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your personal information when you use the Program of Study (POS) Generation System.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide when registering and using the system, including your name, SR-Code, email address (e.g. @g.batstate-u.edu.ph), year level, and academic program. We also collect data related to your courses, grades, and generated Program of Study for academic and administrative purposes.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to generate and manage your Program of Study, communicate with you about your academic records, and improve our services. We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Access to your data is restricted to authorized university personnel and systems necessary for academic and administrative functions.

Data Retention

We retain your data in accordance with university policies and applicable laws. Academic records may be kept as required for accreditation, auditing, and your continued use of the system.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data where permitted by university policy and law. For such requests, please contact the Registrar's Office or the system administrator.

Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the system after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or the Program of Study Generation System, please contact Batangas State University at pos-support@g.batstate-u.edu.ph or visit the Contact Us page.
